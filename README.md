# HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard — Excel

## Project Overview
An Excel-based HR analytics dashboard designed to summarize workforce composition and support people-related business reporting. The project explores employee distribution, salary, performance, attendance, hiring trends, and employee status.

## Business Objectives
- Understand workforce distribution across departments and locations.
- Review employee composition by job level and gender.
- Compare average salary, performance, and attendance across departments.
- Explore employee joining trends over time.
- Monitor employee status to support workforce planning.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query for data preparation
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Dashboard layout and data visualisation

## Dashboard Analysis
The dashboard includes views for:
- Employees by department
- Employees by job level
- Gender distribution
- Employees by location
- Average salary by department
- Average performance by department
- Average attendance by department
- Employees joined by year
- Employee status

## Business Insights
- Operations is the largest department in the sample.
- The gender distribution is approximately balanced.
- Hyderabad has the largest workforce concentration in the sample.
- Salary differences should be interpreted alongside job level and role composition.
- Performance and attendance differences are descriptive and may require further investigation.
- Hiring trends should be compared using complete-year data; partial-year figures can be misleading.
